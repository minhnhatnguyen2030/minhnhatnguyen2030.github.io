# Research image replacement guide

The package deliberately includes **placeholder JPGs** instead of copying figures directly from confidential / submitted coursework files.

Replace any placeholder by uploading a real image with the **same filename**. No HTML editing is then needed.

## Image preparation

For all replacements:
- JPG or PNG is fine.
- Aim for 1200–1800 px on the long edge.
- Crop away unnecessary slide borders and tiny citation text.
- Keep the main graphical message readable on a phone.
- Avoid screenshots containing private names, chats, emails, or other personal data.
- Do not upload the confidential SCDL3991 report PDF to the public repository.
