Summer School 2026 · Monash University

AMSI Summer School 2026 ran from 12 January to 6 February 2026 at Monash University's Clayton campus and online. The official AMSI course titles I use here are:

COURSE 01

Mathematical Optimisation Theory and Applications

Taught by Dr Hoa T. Bui and Dr Vinesha Peiris from Curtin University. The course covered convex optimisation, integer-programming duality, algorithms motivating convex optimisation, approximation theory and applications including neural networks.

COURSE 02

Machine Learning in Financial Mathematics

Taught by Dr Kihun Nam of Monash University. His research sits across stochastic analysis, financial mathematics, optimal control and machine-learning methods for mathematical problems.

Summer School 2025

JAN 2025

Machine Learning and Data Science

Taught by Prof. Yiming Ying of the University of Sydney. The official AMSI pre-enrolment material covers optimisation for machine learning, maximum likelihood, support-vector geometry, KL divergence and related mathematical foundations.

Yiming Ying is Professor in Statistical Data Science at the University of Sydney, with research interests including statistical learning theory, machine learning, optimisation for machine learning and trustworthy AI.

Why AMSI fits my research path

These summer subjects sit between conventional coursework and research training. They gave me a way to strengthen the mathematical language behind optimisation and machine learning while learning alongside honours, postgraduate and early-career researchers from across Australia and New Zealand.
