## Selected Research

### Geometry-Driven Benchmarking of Optimisers for Score Learning
**SCDL3991 · Individual Research · University of Sydney · 2025**  
**Supervisor:** Dr Andi Han

A controlled PyTorch study of optimisation behaviour in diffusion-inspired score learning using Gaussian Mixture Models.

Highlights:

- compared six optimisers across more than **10,000 experimental runs**
- studied the effects of mixture geometry, diffusion noise, and sample size
- found that **geometric asymmetry and curvature anisotropy** were stronger drivers of optimisation difficulty than mode count alone
- **HD — 88/100**

### Image21 — Deep Learning for Breast Cancer Histopathology Analysis
**DATA3888 · Data Science Capstone · 2026**

Interdisciplinary deep-learning project for H&E histopathology image analysis.

Highlights:

- team and technical leadership
- comparison of five CNN architectures
- ResNet18 achieved strong test performance
- EfficientNetB0 selected for deployment practicality
- Grad-CAM interpretability and interactive application
- **HD — 88/100**

## Evidence & Accuracy

The site is written as an **evidence-first academic portfolio**.

Where relevant:

- completed work is distinguished from ongoing work
- proposed research is labelled as proposed / in discussion
- upcoming conferences are marked as upcoming
- visiting-study course enrolments are not presented as completed graded coursework unless supported
- private reports and transcripts are not stored publicly
- unofficial GPA conversions are not presented as official university or KAIST grades
- project roles are described conservatively unless formal evidence supports a stronger title

---

## Privacy

Some supporting documents contain student identifiers, private contact details, or information about other students.

For that reason:

- raw academic transcripts are not hosted publicly
- private research reports are available upon request
- selected screenshots may be cropped or redacted before publication
- public pages prioritise evidence without exposing unnecessary personal data
