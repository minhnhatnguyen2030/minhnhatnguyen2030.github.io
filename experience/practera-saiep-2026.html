Industry project

The Study Australia Industry Experience Program brings together multidisciplinary tertiary student teams to work on real projects for Australian and global employers. Our project was delivered virtually through Practera, combining client-facing work with independent analysis, team collaboration, feedback and a final set of recommendations.

The compressed project format made the work feel very different from a semester-long academic assignment: we had to understand the client's problem quickly, divide responsibilities, gather useful evidence and turn that evidence into recommendations that were concise enough to act on.

My role — Social Growth & Engagement Strategist

My designated role focused on social growth and engagement. I contributed analytical and data-informed thinking to questions around audience growth, engagement and digital positioning while collaborating with teammates responsible for digital strategy and analytics, monetisation and partnerships, and social-media audit work.

This was especially useful for me because the goal was not a model, proof or grade. The analysis had to become a business decision: evidence needed to be translated into recommendations that an external client could understand, critique and potentially use.

From data to decisions

The experience strengthened my ability to work under a client-oriented timeline, communicate across disciplines and translate analysis into business recommendations. It was an early opportunity to apply the quantitative mindset I was developing through Statistics and Data Science in a context where usefulness depended on communication and actionability, not methodological sophistication alone.

Key takeaway

Good analysis is only valuable when it can be translated into a decision someone can act on.
