SCDL3991 · INDIVIDUAL RESEARCH · 2025

HD · 88/100

Geometry-Driven Benchmarking of Optimisers for Score Learning

Semester-long individual research on diffusion-inspired score learning using Gaussian Mixture Models as a controlled testbed. I built a reproducible PyTorch benchmark comparing six optimisers across mixture geometry, diffusion noise and sample size, with more than 10,000 experimental runs. The central finding was that geometric asymmetry and curvature anisotropy were stronger drivers of optimisation difficulty than mode count alone.

Supervisor: Dr Andi Han ↗ — University of Sydney Mathematics & Statistics; research in large generative models, manifold optimisation, efficient foundation models and graph neural networks, with multiple recent papers at ICML and other top ML venues.

DATA3888 · DATA SCIENCE CAPSTONE · 2026

HD · 88/100

Image21 — Deep Learning for Accessible Breast Cancer Histopathology Analysis

As team and technical lead, I helped coordinate Image21, a breast-cancer H&E histopathology project comparing five CNN architectures and integrating an interpretable Shiny prototype. ResNet18 achieved 88.65% test accuracy with macro-F1 0.8796, while EfficientNetB0 was selected for live deployment because it offered a strong accuracy–practicality trade-off with Grad-CAM visualisation.

Research mentor: Manoj M. Wagle ↗ — researcher working across the University of Sydney and MIT CSAIL, with interests in interpretable deep learning, biomedical AI, neuroscience and computational biology.

Project materials available upon request; research and education prototype, not a clinical diagnostic tool.

PROPOSED HONOURS RESEARCH · 2026

IN DISCUSSION

Sparse Sufficient Dimension Reduction via Distance Covariance

Research direction currently under discussion with Dr Linh Nghiem and Dr Andi Han. The proposed project studies sufficient dimension reduction through distance covariance, then extends the framework to impose structure such as sparsity while developing efficient manifold optimisation algorithms for high-dimensional data.

Academic preparation: I have studied with Dr Nghiem in MATH1962 Mathematics 1B (Advanced) and STAT3922 Applied Linear Models (Advanced), giving this prospective project a direct link to my advanced mathematical-statistical training.

Generative AI Readiness in Teacher Education: A Mixed-Methods Study of Pre-Service Teachers’ Competencies, Strategies, and Support Needs
